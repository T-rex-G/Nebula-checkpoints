# Nebulaverse-X Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Secure Access and Policy Digital Twin

**Status:** Closed as a qualified alpha checkpoint in `5.3.0-alpha.16.3`.

Tasks 1–21 delivered and qualified the secure access, central mutation,
governance, evidence, recovery, and provider-compatibility foundation. This
closure is not a production-readiness or hosted-public-alpha claim.

## Controlled hosted public-alpha readiness

**Status:** In progress for `5.3.0-alpha.17.0`.

The successor programme establishes product truth, controlled invitation and
sandbox enforcement, credential and privacy lifecycle controls, capability-aware
UX, Render/Neon operations, and exact-candidate qualification. The cohort
remains closed until all release gates pass.

## Phase 2 — Repository Trust Digital Twin

**Status:** Planned after public-alpha readiness.

Model repository trust posture, controls, permissions, CI/CD, dependencies,
security configuration, governance drift, evidence, and explainable scoring.

## Phase 3 — Verified Recovery Game Day

**Status:** Planned after Phase 2.

Exercise restore workflows, measure recovery point and time, verify results,
record evidence, and track remediation.

## Phase 4 — Organization-Wide Intelligence

**Status:** Planned after Phase 3.

Add multi-repository governance visibility, inherited policies, portfolio
analysis, trends, reporting, and prioritised remediation.

## Phase 5 — Customer-Controlled Evidence Retention

**Status:** Accepted later-phase direction, planned after Phase 4.

Persist the stable signed evidence envelopes from Phase 1 to customer-controlled
S3-compatible storage under customer credentials, region, and retention policy.
This is not implemented in the current successor.
